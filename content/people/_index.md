## PhD Students

    <h3>Ayse Coban</h3>
    <p class="person-title">PhD Student</p>
    <p>
      Ayse is a PhD student at LMU Klinikum and Helmholtz Munich, where she is developing single-cell long-read multiomic
      approaches to study RNA-driven cellular plasticity in pediatric malignancies. She completed her bachelor’s degree in
      Molecular and Cell Biology with an emphasis in Biochemistry, Biophysics and Structural Biology at UC Berkeley, followed
      by research experience at the Lilly Biotechnology Center in San Diego. She later earned her M.Sc. in Molecular and
      Cellular Biology from LMU Munich. During her master’s, she also worked at Roche Diagnostics on the Sequencing by
      Expansion (SBX) project, contributing to the development of a novel next-generation sequencing technology. Her research
      interests span RNA biology, sequencing technologies, single-cell genomics, and tumor heterogeneity. Outside the lab,
      Ayse likes exploring different parts of Germany, particularly its castles, lakes, and Alpine regions.
    </p>
  </article>

    <h3>Keito Taketomi</h3>
    <p class="person-title">PhD Student</p>
    <p>
      I grew up in Yamanashi, a mountainous prefecture west of Tokyo, Japan. I did my bachelor's in applied mathematics and
      my master's in statistics at Columbia University in New York, and later worked on cancer genomics at the University of
      Cambridge in the UK. I came to statistics because I care about good medical evidence reaching the people who need it.
      For my PhD, I investigate RNA splicing and regulation in pediatric disease by applying machine learning to single-cell
      long-read sequencing data. Outside the lab, I like exploring cities across Germany and the rest of Europe.
    </p>
