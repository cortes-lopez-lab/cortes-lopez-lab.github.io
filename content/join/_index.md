We bring together experimental and computational scientists to study RNA regulation in pediatric disease. If our work
resonates with you, we would love to hear from you, even when no position is advertised.

## Who we welcome

- **Experimental technicians** with hands-on experience in molecular biology, cell culture, or single-cell and sequencing workflows
- **Master students** looking for thesis projects
- **Bachelor students and interns**

## How to apply

Email a short statement of interests, your CV, and references to [Mariela.Cortes@med.uni-muenchen.de](mailto:Mariela.Cortes@med.uni-muenchen.de) or [mariela.corteslopez@helmholtz-munich.de](mailto:mariela.corteslopez@helmholtz-munich.de).
